About the team

Not real fast.
Still here.

Half Fast Racing is a 24 Hours of Lemons endurance team. We race cars that cost $500 or less, for a whole weekend at a time, against a hundred others.

The short version

Lemons is endurance racing for cars nobody else wanted. You buy one for $500 or less, bolt in a roll cage, and try to keep it running for two days. Finishing is an achievement. Finishing well is suspicious.

We've been at it for more than ten years. In 2026 we ran 12 race weekends, put 11 different cars on track and finished on the overall podium in Texas. We're second in the national points and leading the South.

The team's own story goes here: how it started, the first car, the worst weekend. The owner writes or dictates it from the admin page.
